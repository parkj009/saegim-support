Saegim Support

Need help with the app or its widgets? Start with the answers below. If your issue continues, contact support through GitHub.

Contact support
Open a support request on GitHub. Please describe what happened and include your iOS version. Do not include private information.

Today’s word is not changing

Saegim selects one word for each calendar day. Close and reopen the app after midnight. If the previous word remains, restart your iPhone and open Saegim again.

A widget is blank or out of date

Open Saegim once, then return to the Home Screen or Lock Screen. If needed, remove the widget and add it again from the widget gallery.

How do I add a Home Screen widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen, choose Edit, select Add Widget, search for Saegim, choose a widget, and tap Add Widget.

How do I add a Lock Screen widget?

Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, select Lock Screen, tap the widget area, and choose Saegim.

Does Saegim require an account or internet connection?

No. Saegim works offline and does not require an account.
