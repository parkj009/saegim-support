Privacy Policy

Effective September 20, 2026

Saegim is designed to work without collecting your personal information.

Information Saegim collects

Saegim does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal data. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or in-app purchases.

How the app works

Saegim’s vocabulary and examples are bundled with the app. Daily words and recent-word history are generated on your device. The app does not send this activity to the developer or to third parties.

Widgets

Saegim’s Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets use information supplied locally by the app to display the daily word. This information remains on your device.

Third-party services

Saegim does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, authentication, or cloud-storage services. Apple may process information when you download or update the app under Apple’s own privacy policy.

Children’s privacy

Because Saegim does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If Saegim’s features or data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect. The effective date above will identify the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, open a request through the Saegim support repository. Please do not include sensitive personal information.
